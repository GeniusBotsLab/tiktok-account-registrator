## Product overview

**TikTok Account Registrator** is a commercial solution for permitted browser workflows involving TikTok accounts that the customer owns or is authorised to administer. It supports controlled operational processes that need organised service data, automation profiles, content operations, and action history.

## Capabilities

- Browser workflows for authorised-account operations.
- Controlled organisation of operational data and automation profiles.
- ZennoPoster profile support for permitted scenarios.
- Configurable workflows for publishing owned content, scheduling, inbound-comment handling, and business communications.
- Status monitoring, reporting preparation, and custom adaptation to an agreed workflow.
